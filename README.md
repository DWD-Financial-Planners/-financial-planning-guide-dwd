# 💰 When Is the Right Time to See a Financial Advisor?

## A Practical Guide to Financial Planning at Every Stage of Life

Many people assume that you only need a financial advisor when you have accumulated significant wealth or are approaching retirement.

That's not necessarily the case.

Your financial priorities change throughout your life. The decisions you make in your twenties can influence your financial position in your thirties and forties, while decisions made before retirement can have a significant impact on your future income and lifestyle.

At **DWD Financial Planners**, we believe financial planning should be about more than numbers. It's about understanding your circumstances, your goals, your responsibilities, and the future you want to build.

---

## 🧠 Why Financial Planning Matters at Every Age

Financial planning isn't simply about choosing financial products.

It's about creating a strategy that helps you make informed decisions about your money and your future.

Depending on your circumstances, financial planning may involve:

✔ Setting realistic financial goals  
✔ Creating a savings strategy  
✔ Planning for retirement  
✔ Managing financial risks  
✔ Protecting your income and family  
✔ Managing your wealth  
✔ Considering tax-efficient strategies  
✔ Planning your estate  
✔ Reviewing your retirement funds  
✔ Preparing for changes in your financial circumstances  

The earlier you understand your financial position, the more opportunities you may have to make thoughtful long-term decisions.

---

# 👤 Financial Planning in Your 20s

Your twenties are often the beginning of your independent financial life.

You may be starting your first full-time job, building your career, paying off debt, saving for your first major purchase, or simply learning how to manage your income.

Retirement can seem a long way away.

But this can be a valuable time to establish good financial habits.

## What can you focus on?

✔ Creating a realistic budget  
✔ Building an emergency fund  
✔ Starting retirement planning  
✔ Understanding your financial protection needs  
✔ Setting short- and long-term financial goals  
✔ Learning how different financial strategies work  

Starting early gives your financial strategy more time to develop.

You don't necessarily need to have a large amount of money before beginning financial planning.

What matters is having a clear understanding of where you are and where you want to go.

---

# 👨‍👩‍👧 Financial Planning in Your 30s

Your thirties can bring significant changes.

You may get married, buy a home, have children, advance your career, start a business, or take on additional financial responsibilities.

As your responsibilities grow, your financial decisions can become more complicated.

This is often a good time to review whether your financial strategy still reflects your current circumstances.

## Areas worth reviewing include:

✔ Retirement contributions  
✔ Life and disability cover  
✔ Emergency savings  
✔ Children's future financial needs  
✔ Wealth-building strategies  
✔ Tax planning  
✔ Debt management  
✔ Short- and long-term financial goals  

A financial planner can help you consider how these different priorities fit together rather than treating each financial decision separately.

---

# 📈 Financial Planning in Your 40s

By your forties, you may have accumulated assets, increased your income, and taken on greater financial responsibilities.

This can be an important stage for reviewing the bigger financial picture.

You may start asking questions such as:

> Am I on track for retirement?

> Are my assets structured appropriately?

> Is my family adequately protected?

> Are there unnecessary financial risks I should address?

> Could my tax position be improved?

> Have I started planning my estate?

These questions don't have identical answers for everyone.

Your income, assets, family circumstances, responsibilities, risk exposure, and long-term objectives all need to be considered.

## Financial planning at this stage may include:

✔ Wealth management  
✔ Retirement planning reviews  
✔ Estate planning  
✔ Tax planning  
✔ Risk management  
✔ Life and disability cover  
✔ Long-term financial goal reviews  

The objective is not simply to accumulate wealth.

It's also about protecting what you've built and ensuring your financial strategy continues to support your future.

---

# 🏖️ Financial Planning in Your 50s and Beyond

As retirement approaches, financial planning can become increasingly important.

Your focus may shift from accumulating wealth to understanding how your accumulated resources can support your retirement lifestyle.

This can involve reviewing retirement funds, expected income, expenses, tax considerations, estate planning, and potential retirement income solutions.

## Retirement planning may involve:

✔ Assessing your retirement readiness  
✔ Reviewing pension and retirement funds  
✔ Considering retirement income requirements  
✔ Reviewing living annuity options  
✔ Considering tax implications  
✔ Reviewing estate and legacy planning  
✔ Assessing your ongoing wealth management needs  

Retirement planning isn't simply about reaching a particular savings amount.

It's about understanding how your available resources can work together to support your needs throughout retirement.

---

# 🛡️ Don't Wait for a Financial Crisis

A common misconception is that you only need financial advice when something goes wrong.

But professional financial planning can also be useful when things are going well.

You may want guidance when:

✔ Starting your first job  
✔ Getting married  
✔ Having children  
✔ Buying a property  
✔ Changing careers  
✔ Receiving an inheritance  
✔ Starting a business  
✔ Building significant wealth  
✔ Approaching retirement  
✔ Reviewing your existing financial arrangements  

Major life changes can create new financial responsibilities and decisions.

Having a plan can help you approach those decisions with greater clarity.

Financial advice can cover much more than investments.

## Retirement Planning

Helping you structure and manage your wealth in line with your longer-term financial objectives.

Considering tax-free savings options as part of a broader financial strategy.

# 🚨 Signs It May Be Time to Speak to a Financial Planner

There isn't a specific age at which everyone should see a financial advisor.

However, professional guidance may be worth considering if:

✔ You don't have a clear financial plan  
✔ You're unsure how much you should be saving for retirement  
✔ You have several financial goals competing for your attention  
✔ You want to review your wealth strategy  
✔ You need to assess your financial protection  
✔ You're approaching retirement  
✔ You have recently experienced a major life change  
✔ You need assistance with tax or estate planning  
✔ You have accumulated significant assets  
✔ You're unsure whether your current financial arrangements remain appropriate

# 📊 Financial Planning Is About the Bigger Picture

One financial decision can affect several areas of your financial life.

For example, retirement planning may involve considerations around:

**Retirement funds → Tax → Income → Wealth management → Estate planning**

Similarly, protecting your family may involve:

**Income → Life cover → Disability cover → Risk management → Estate planning**

This is why looking at your financial circumstances as a whole can be valuable.

A financial strategy should reflect your individual circumstances rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all approach.
